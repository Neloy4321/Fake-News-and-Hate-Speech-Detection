The seven-tab workbook includes: `Reviewer_Readme`, `Master_360`, `Binary_Eligible_317`, `Exceptions_43`, `Raw_Provenance_360`, `Agreement_Audit`, and `Protocol_Codebook`.

### Locked numbers

- **360** original audited IDs, unique, with full Bengali article text.
- **360/360** original Neloy confidence and **360/360** original Sajib confidence, on the 1–5 ordinal scale.
- A *derived* minimum of those two confidence ratings is available on all 360 rows. This is not an independent human confidence score.
- Evidence-review confidence is available on the **57** disputed cases only; no missing values for 303 agreed cases are fabricated.
- **317** strict binary-eligible author-approved cases, 223 REAL and 94 FAKE.
- **43** cases not assigned strict full-article binary ground truth: 5 conditional central-claim, 13 hold, 25 not-news.
- Original raw inter-annotator label equality 303/360. The two original annotation schemes differed, so subset κ must not be presented as whole-sample reliability.

### Provenance

The 57 disputed dispositions were accepted by the author after AI-assisted review. An author-approved disposition is **not** a separate independent human source verification. Original independent annotator labels, confidence, notes, dates and source links remain visible. The companion corrected-label evaluation confirms all 360 IDs against saved original test rows but does not claim clean retraining.
