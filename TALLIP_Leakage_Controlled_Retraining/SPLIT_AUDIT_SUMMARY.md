### Prior-evidence discrepancies retained and disclosed

The earlier audit proposed additional 'exact overlap' train exclusions that a fresh independent normalization of the original CSV did **not** reproduce:

- Fake News train original row IDs (zero-based): `22799`, `35758`, `64621` — 3 rows.
- Hate Speech train original row IDs (zero-based): `6485`, `7011`, `8892`, `13834`, `22685` — 5 rows.

All eight rows were **conservatively excluded**, marked as `prior_exclusion_not_independently_exact`; they are not falsely labeled as independently confirmed duplicates.

Three prior Hate Speech test-near candidates had **prior Jaccard = 1.0** but yielded a different score using raw-CSV text normalization, possibly because the prior audit used an additional Bengali normalization step. Candidate rows (`train:7011` vs `test:1322`; `train:13834` vs `test:2063`; `train:6485` vs `test:2796`) are excluded conservatively and the prior and recomputed scores are recorded exactly in the JSON manifest.

Three Hate Speech training comments normalized to an empty string (punctuation/symbol-only) and were removed; no original test item was removed or altered.

## Reproducibility evidence and scientific limits

1. `evidence/clean_split_manifest.json` records the SHA-256 of the **two original dataset ZIPs**, minimal prior evidence ZIP, all six locally generated compressed clean CSVs, and a fixed test-text digest per task.
2. `evidence/*_removed_row_ids.csv` records every removed original row ID and all applicable reasons, without reproducing news articles or hate-speech text.
3. `evidence/12_run_plan_verified.json` confirms the plan for **two tasks × two base models × three seeds**. Its status is `PLAN_ONLY`; it reports **no model metrics**.
4. `tests/test_cleaning.py` passed five unit checks; cleaned source CSVs passed an independent row-count/hash verification via `train_eval.py --plan-only`.
5. The screening and cleanup **cannot prove all possible semantic or approximate cross-split duplicates are absent**. Furthermore, deleting earlier data based on a fixed test comparison is retrospective. These are limitations to state explicitly to reviewers.
